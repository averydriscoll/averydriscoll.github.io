Beyond peer-reviewed research, I work to make climate and agricultural science useful and accessible to broader audiences.

## Climate solutions at Project Drawdown

I work with an interdisciplinary team at [Project Drawdown](https://drawdown.org/explorer) to develop the Drawdown Explorer, a public-facing library of climate solutions, with a focus on nutrient management and ecosystem protection.

## Consulting

I am available for consulting related to greenhouse gas emissions accounting and mitigation, particularly in the context of agriculture and land use change. Please [reach out](mailto:avery.driscoll@colostate.edu) to discuss.

## Teaching and mentoring

My work on crop diversity and yield stability grew out of a Colorado State University course in environmental data science and was ultimately co-authored by every student in the class — an approach to research training I care about and hope to continue.
