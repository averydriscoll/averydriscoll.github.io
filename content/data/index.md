I am committed to open, reusable science. The datasets and code below are freely available, and I welcome inquiries from anyone interested in building on them.

## Greenhouse gas emissions from US irrigation

County-scale estimates of direct greenhouse gas emissions from irrigation across the United States, spanning energy use, soil denitrification, and groundwater degassing.

- Data for *Nature Communications* (2024) — irrigation pumping energy use: [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/10416689)
- Data for *Nature Water* (2024) — total direct emissions: [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.12552398)

## Crop diversity and yield stability

Harmonized and gap-filled USDA Census data on the area and production of 90 crops across US states from 1981–2020, along with the analysis code.

- Data and code: [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/7332106)

## Desert plant ecophysiology

A long-term monitoring record of desert shrub growth, survival, and leaf stable-isotope chemistry, begun by Dr. Jim Ehleringer in the early 1980s and continued today. An interactive web app supports student exploration of the database.

- Explore the database: [web app](https://www.stevekannenberg.com/about-1)
