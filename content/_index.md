I am a researcher working at the intersection of agriculture, climate change, and data science. The core goal of my work is to identify effective and scalable ways for food systems to respond to a changing climate — quantifying how agricultural management and policy shape both crop production and environmental impacts, especially greenhouse gas emissions.

I recently completed my PhD in Soil and Crop Science at Colorado State University and am joining the [Purdue Agroecology Laboratory](https://ag.purdue.edu/department/agry/lab-sites/pal/index.html) as a postdoc in early 2026. I also work with [Project Drawdown](https://drawdown.org/explorer) on their public library of climate solutions.
